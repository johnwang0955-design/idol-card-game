# Third-party assets & licenses (2.5D build)

## Art
- Idols, outfits (32 items), portraits: original illustrated layers made for this project (no third-party characters or clothing).
- The main idol is a 2D "paper puppet": the layers are warped by a small 2D skeleton at runtime. No 3D model / VRM file is used in this build.

## Everything else
Stage, runway, mirror gate, audience, penlights, sparkles, music and UI: made in code, original.
